Act III: The Colleges

Five Indian institutions with strong English programs. Scroll sideways through the company.

Delhi

St. Stephen’s College

University of Delhi. Widely recognised as one of India’s top institutions for English Studies. Known for a rigorous critical theory curriculum, vibrant literary societies, and graduates in academia, civil services and journalism.

Mumbai

St. Xavier’s College

Autonomous. A historic premier institute for humanities and liberal arts, covering literary history, contemporary postcolonial literature, creative writing and media studies.

Kolkata

Presidency University

Formerly Presidency College, with a legacy in Indian higher education. Its English department emphasises literary criticism, comparative literature and cultural analysis.

Delhi

Lady Shri Ram College

University of Delhi. A leading liberal arts college for women in South Asia, focused on feminist literary theory, world literature, rhetoric and modern literary studies.

Hyderabad

EFLU

The English and Foreign Languages University is a Central University devoted to English and foreign languages, with degrees in literature, linguistics, English Language Teaching, and media and communication.
